What the numbers mean

Accepted
A reviewer saved the label the auditor suggested, on the suggested text.
Rejected
A reviewer saved another label on a suggested text, or removed the suggested label.
Missed
A reviewer labeled text the auditor had not suggested.
Precision
Accepted ÷ (accepted + rejected): how often a suggestion the reviewer acted on was right.
Coverage
Accepted ÷ (accepted + missed): how many of the reviewers' labels the auditor had already suggested.
Not counted
Suggestions a reviewer simply ignored. Each text counts once per reviewer and task. Only counts and hashes are shared: no document text.
